ℹ️ About This Tool

Understanding and valuing nature's contributions to human well-being.

🎯 What is GEP?

Gross Ecosystem Product (GEP) is the total monetary value of ecosystem services provided by nature. It is divided into four categories:

  • Provisioning (EPV) – food, water, timber, fisheries, wild resources
  • Regulating (ERV) – carbon sequestration, air/water purification, flood control, pollination
  • Cultural (ECV) – ecotourism, recreation, aesthetic, spiritual, educational
  • Fauna (FV) – wildlife tourism, animal products, genetic resources, pest control, seed dispersal

Formula: GEP = EPV + ERV + ECV + FV
